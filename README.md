# 🚀 Hackathon Projects

**Real-world challenges. Shared ideas. Learning by building.**

## About Hackathons

Hackathons bring people with different skills and perspectives together to tackle a challenge within a limited time. They create space to exchange ideas, experiment, and turn a problem into a practical solution.

This portfolio collects my collaborative hackathon experiences while representing **42 Heilbronn**, with room for new events and lessons along the way.

## 🎯 Goals & Motivation

I take part in hackathons to:

- Explore **real-world problems** and understand what people need.
- Put **software development and AI** into practice.
- Develop **product thinking** and try new ideas.
- Work with teammates across disciplines and learn from their perspectives.
- Learn through hands-on experimentation.
- Practice clear communication and make decisions together under time pressure.

## 💡 What I Take Away

For me, a hackathon is about more than winning. I value the whole process: understanding a problem, exchanging perspectives, developing a solution, and learning from teammates.

I want to leave each event with more experience, better questions, and a clearer sense of how to build something useful together.
